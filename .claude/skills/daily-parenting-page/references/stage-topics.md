# Topics by stage

A menu, not a script. Prefer what's useful now or within the next 2–6 weeks; mix the
topic tags across the week. Each line is a direction to research, not a fact to cite.

## Second trimester (weeks 14–27): tags `mid`, `wN`

- health: anatomy ultrasound; glucose screening; feeling first movements; sleep positions;
  dental care; travel; warning signs; flu, Tdap, RSV vaccine timing; exercise.
- education: how the baby's senses develop; reading and talking to the bump; choosing a
  childbirth class; how labor works (overview).
- mind: stress and mood in pregnancy, perinatal depression screening, partner
  communication, sleep.
- growth: fetal development milestones by week.
- resources: choosing a pediatrician; NY Paid Family Leave and employer leave timelines;
  insurance and breast pump coverage; NYC childcare options and waitlists (daycare,
  nanny, nanny share); registry essentials; car seat basics.

## Third trimester (weeks 28–40): tags `late`, `wN`

- health: kick counts and fetal movement; preeclampsia signs; group B strep test; when to
  go to the hospital; birth plan; postpartum recovery basics.
- education: newborn sleep safety (ABCs), feeding plans, skin-to-skin, newborn screening.
- mind: preparing for the postpartum period; dividing night duty; asking for help.
- growth: what the newborn can see and hear.
- resources: hospital bag; installing and inspecting the car seat; setting up a safe
  sleep space; pumping basics; postpartum support in NYC.

## Newborn (0–3 months): tag `newborn`

Feeding and growth, safe sleep, crying and soothing, jaundice, fever thresholds, vaccines
schedule, tummy time, postpartum mood, returning to work, childcare start.

## Infant (3–12 months): tag `infant`

Developmental milestones (CDC), starting solids, allergen introduction, sleep, teething,
babyproofing, language through talking and reading, choking safety.

## Toddler (1–3 years): tag `toddler`

Language growth, play, tantrums and emotions, screen time, nutrition, preschool choices.
