When you're admitted to give birth, most hospitals ask for your pediatrician's name, and the delivery nurse calls that pediatrician or the on-call associate as soon as the baby is born.1

Things move quickly after discharge too: the AAP recommends a checkup within 48 to 72 hours after the newborn leaves the hospital, which matters most for breastfed babies.1

That's why the AAP recommends that all parents-to-be meet a pediatrician during the third trimester, to start a trusted relationship.2 The second trimester is a good time to build the shortlist and check insurance, then book the meeting later.

第二步・原理第二步・原理Step 2 · Why

每一件事，都多問一句「為什麼」每一件事，都多问一句“为什么”One more "why" for each

寶寶還沒出生，為什麼現在就要想這件事？宝宝还没出生，为什么现在就要想这件事？The baby isn't here yet. Why think about this now?

因為醫師在你還沒出院前就會出場。沒有併發症時，例行新生兒檢查可在出生後 24 小時內任何時間做；住院期間很多小兒科醫師會每天看寶寶，出院當天做完整檢查，並告訴你何時回診。1

因为医师在你还没出院前就会出场。没有并发症时，例行新生儿检查可在出生后 24 小时内任何时间做；住院期间很多儿科医生会每天看宝宝，出院当天做完整检查，并告诉你何时复诊。1

Because the doctor shows up before you even go home. Without complications, the routine newborn exam can happen any time in the first 24 hours. Many pediatricians check the baby daily in the hospital, do a full exam on discharge day and tell you when to schedule the first office visit.1

為什麼出院後幾天就要回診？为什么出院后几天就要复诊？Why a checkup only days after going home?

因為頭幾天要確認寶寶吃得夠。AAP 給醫師的指引說，這次門診要仔細評估營養，特別是出院前母乳還沒順利建立的寶寶；體重比出生時掉超過 10% 需要進一步評估，而出生體重通常要 7 到 14 天才回來。4

因为头几天要确认宝宝吃得够。AAP 给医师的指引说，这次门诊要仔细评估营养，特别是出院前母乳还没顺利创建的宝宝；体重比出生时掉超过 10% 需要进一步评估，而出生体重通常要 7 到 14 天才回来。4

Because the first days are about making sure the baby is eating enough. AAP guidance for clinicians says this visit should include a careful nutrition check, especially if breastfeeding wasn't well established before discharge. Losing more than 10% of birth weight needs further evaluation, and birth weight usually isn't regained until 7 to 14 days.4

同一份指引也提到要看黃疸（皮膚變黃），黃疸常和脫水有關；出生幾天後，尿和大便的次數則用來判斷寶寶奶量夠不夠。4

同一份指引也提到要看黄疸（皮肤变黄），黄疸常和脱水有关；出生几天后，尿和大便的次数则用来判断宝宝奶量够不够。4

The same guidance lists jaundice (yellowing skin), which it says is often linked with dehydration, and, after the first few days, uses pee and poop patterns to judge whether the baby is getting enough milk.4

This doctor stays with your child for years and looks out for the whole family. The AAP says a prenatal visit can cover car seats, what goes in a crib or bassinet, which vaccines family members need to protect the newborn, breastfeeding and recognizing postpartum depression.2 It's also the only routine AAP wellness visit where the child doesn't need to be in the room.2

AAP 的建議時間是孕晚期，沒有寫原因。AAP 的頁面分別寫「第三孕期」和「懷孕的最後幾個月」，沒有給週數，也沒說明為什麼選這段時間。12

The AAP says third trimester, without giving a reason. Its pages say "the third trimester" and "the final months of your pregnancy," with no week numbers and no stated reason for that window.12

我們讀到的 AAP 頁面沒有給成效數字。AAP 說明它的目的是打好信任關係的基礎，並提到第一胎、高風險妊娠、有併發症、懷多胞胎或領養的家庭特別受益，但沒有列出研究結果。2

我们读到的 AAP 页面没有给成效数字。AAP 说明它的目的是打好信任关系的基础，并提到第一胎、高风险妊娠、有并发症、怀多胞胎或领养的家庭特别受益，但没有列出研究结果。2

The AAP pages we read don't give outcome numbers. The AAP describes the goal as laying a foundation for a trusting relationship, and says first-time parents, high-risk or complicated pregnancies, multiples and adoptions benefit most, but it doesn't cite measured results.2
