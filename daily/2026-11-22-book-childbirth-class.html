生产课会教产兆（快要生了的征兆）、产程的各个阶段、待产姿势、减痛方法和应对宫缩的技巧，而且会在课堂上实际练习；很多课也会带你参观产房。1

A childbirth class covers the signs of labor, the stages of labor, positions, ways to manage pain and techniques for coping with contractions, which you practise in class. Many also include a tour of the birthing facility.1
