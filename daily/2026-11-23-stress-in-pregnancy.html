因為壓力會改變身體和行為，不只是心情。NICHD 指出，太多壓力會讓人睡不好、頭痛、沒胃口或吃太多，這些都可能對媽媽和寶寶不好；高壓力也可能造成高血壓，提高早產或低出生體重的機會。1

Because stress changes your body and your habits, not just your mood. NICHD says too much stress can cause trouble sleeping, headaches, loss of appetite or overeating, all of which can be harmful, and high stress can raise blood pressure, which increases the chance of preterm labor or a low-birthweight baby.1

還有其他路徑，但科學還沒完全講清楚。March of Dimes 說，嚴重或長期的壓力可能影響免疫系統，增加感染風險，而某些感染會提高早產風險；有些人會用抽菸、喝酒或藥物來應付壓力，這本身就會傷害母嬰。某些和壓力有關的荷爾蒙可能也有作用，但他們直說「還不完全了解」壓力如何影響懷孕。2

There are other paths, but the science isn't settled. March of Dimes says serious or long-lasting stress may affect the immune system and raise the risk of infection, and some infections raise the risk of preterm birth. Some people cope by smoking, drinking or using drugs, which harms parent and baby. Certain stress-related hormones may also play a role, but March of Dimes says plainly that how stress affects pregnancy isn't completely understood.2

和低出生體重的關聯比較清楚，和早產的關聯結果不一致。一篇 2018 年的統合分析（把多項研究合併計算）納入 8 項世代研究、約 8,271 名孕婦：孕期有壓力的一組，生出低出生體重寶寶的勝算約為對照組的 1.68 倍（95% 信賴區間 1.19–2.38）；早產在主要分析中沒有統計上顯著差異（1.98 倍，區間 0.91–4.31），只有在敏感度分析中顯著（1.42 倍，區間 1.05–1.91）。4

和低出生体重的关联比较清楚，和早产的关联结果不一致。一篇 2018 年的荟萃分析（把多项研究合并计算）纳入 8 项队列研究、约 8,271 名孕妇：孕期有压力的一组，生出低出生体重宝宝的胜算约为对照组的 1.68 倍（95% 置信区间 1.19–2.38）；早产在主要分析中没有统计上显著差异（1.98 倍，区间 0.91–4.31），只有在敏感度分析中显著（1.42 倍，区间 1.05–1.91）。4

The link with low birthweight is clearer; the link with preterm birth is inconsistent. A 2018 meta-analysis pooling 8 cohort studies with about 8,271 pregnant women found that stress in pregnancy was linked to higher odds of low birthweight (odds ratio 1.68, 95% CI 1.19–2.38). For preterm labor, the main analysis found no statistically significant difference (OR 1.98, 95% CI 0.91–4.31); only a sensitivity analysis did (OR 1.42, 95% CI 1.05–1.91).4

這些是「關聯」，不是證明壓力直接造成。作者自己也說，需要樣本更大、追蹤更久的研究來確認。4

These are associations, not proof that stress is the direct cause. The authors themselves call for larger studies with longer follow-up to confirm the findings.4

March of Dimes suggests cutting back on things you don't need to do (for example, asking your partner to take on chores), planning time away with your employer if you work and using time off to relax, accepting help from people you trust, and seeing a social worker for money or other practical problems, along with eating healthy foods, getting plenty of sleep and exercising with your provider's OK.2

對媽媽的壓力和焦慮有幫助，對寶寶的好處證據還有限。2024 年一篇系統性回顧納入 32 項研究、3,979 名孕婦，方法包括瑜伽、音樂、漸進式肌肉放鬆、深呼吸、引導想像、正念和催眠。和對照組相比，壓力量表（PSS）平均低約 4.1 分（9 項試驗），焦慮量表（STAI）低約 5 分，憂鬱量表（EPDS）低約 2.3 分；寶寶出生體重平均多約 80 公克（8 項試驗、1,239 人，區間 1–157 公克）。5

对妈妈的压力和焦虑有帮助，对宝宝的好处证据还有限。2024 年一篇系统性回顾纳入 32 项研究、3,979 名孕妇，方法包括瑜伽、音乐、渐进式肌肉放松、深呼吸、引导想像、正念和催眠。和对照组相比，压力量表（PSS）平均低约 4.1 分（9 项试验），焦虑量表（STAI）低约 5 分，抑郁量表（EPDS）低约 2.3 分；宝宝出生体重平均多约 80 克（8 项试验、1,239 人，区间 1–157 克）。5

They help with the mother's stress and anxiety; the evidence for the baby is thinner. A 2024 systematic review of 32 studies with 3,979 pregnant women covered yoga, music, progressive muscle relaxation, deep breathing, guided imagery, mindfulness and hypnosis. Compared with control groups, stress scores (Perceived Stress Scale) were about 4.1 points lower across 9 trials, anxiety scores (STAI) about 5 points lower and depression scores (EPDS) about 2.3 points lower. Birth weight was about 80 g higher on average (8 trials, 1,239 participants, 95% CI 1–157 g).5

但研究之間差異很大。作者指出，多數試驗原本是為了看媽媽的心理健康而設計，出生體重以外的新生兒結果資料太少，無法合併分析。5

但研究之间差异很大。作者指出，多数试验原本是为了看妈妈的心理健康而设计，出生体重以外的新生儿结果数据太少，无法合并分析。5

But the studies varied a lot. The authors note that most trials were designed to measure the mother's mental health, and data on newborn outcomes other than birth weight were too limited to pool.5
