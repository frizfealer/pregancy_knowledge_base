第一步・白話版第一步・白话版Step 1 · The short version

寶寶睡覺的地方，越空越安全宝宝睡觉的地方，越空越安全The emptier the sleep space, the safer

美國小兒科學會（AAP）建議每次睡眠都讓寶寶仰躺在符合聯邦安全標準的嬰兒床、搖籃或遊戲床等獨立睡眠空間。床墊應堅固、平坦、不傾斜，與床架緊密貼合，只加一張床包；不要放毯子、枕頭、床圍或其他柔軟物品。7144

AAP recommends putting babies on their backs for every sleep in a separate sleep space that meets federal standards, such as a crib, bassinet or play yard. Use a firm, flat, noninclined mattress that fits snugly, with only a fitted sheet; keep blankets, pillows, bumpers and other soft objects out.7144

所以列購物清單時，有些東西應跳過：法律所定義的床圍，以及睡眠表面斜度超過 10 度、供一歲以內嬰兒睡眠使用（按產品預定用途、行銷或設計認定）的斜躺睡床，自 2022 年 11 月 12 日起已是聯邦禁售品。這不是說斜度在 10 度以內的產品就適合睡眠。AAP 建議避開睡眠定位枕、加厚軟床墊、加重毯與加重睡袋，也不要讓寶寶睡在哺乳枕或躺椅墊上。456714

Skip the products covered by the federal ban effective November 12, 2022: legally defined crib bumpers and sleepers with a sleep surface steeper than 10 degrees intended, marketed or designed for infant sleep up to age 1. An incline of 10 degrees or less does not by itself make a product safe for sleep. AAP also advises against positioners, plush mattress toppers, weighted blankets and weighted sleep sacks, and against sleeping on nursing pillows or loungers.456714

第二步・原理第二步・原理Step 2 · Why

每一樣都多問一句「為什麼」每一样都多问一句“为什么”One more "why" for each

床圍不是能防寶寶撞到欄杆嗎？為什麼要禁？床围不是能防宝宝撞到栏杆吗？为什么要禁？Don't bumpers stop the baby from hitting the rails? Why ban them?

因為現在的嬰兒床本來就不需要。AAP 說，安全規定要求欄杆間距很窄，所以床圍沒有必要。聯邦禁令涵蓋用來防撞或阻隔側欄開口以防卡住的材料，包括有襯墊床圍、塑膠床欄護墊和欄杆套。75

Because today's cribs don't need them. AAP explains that narrow rail spacing makes bumpers unnecessary. The federal definition covers material intended to prevent impacts against crib sides or to block openings to prevent entrapment, including padded bumpers, vinyl guards and slat covers.75

那不加襯墊的網布床圍呢？它不在聯邦禁令範圍內，紐約州的床圍禁售規定也排除網布。但法律例外不是安全認證：AAP 建議睡眠空間不使用床圍或附著於床欄的類似產品，以避免窒息、卡住或勒頸。51614

What about non-padded mesh liners? They are excluded from the federal ban and New York's bumper-pad sales restriction. A legal exemption is not a safety endorsement: AAP recommends keeping bumpers and similar crib-side attachments out of the sleep space because of entrapment, suffocation and strangulation risks.51614

寶寶斜躺好像睡得比較好，為什麼不行？宝宝斜躺好像睡得比较好，为什么不行？My baby seems to sleep better propped up. Why not?

因為斜躺睡床和嬰兒死亡有關。寶寶在斜躺姿勢頭部可能向前或側面垂下，阻礙呼吸。嬰兒窩、楔形墊或巢等名稱並不能保證產品安全；選擇符合聯邦嬰兒睡眠產品標準的平坦睡眠空間。714

Because inclined sleepers are linked to infant deaths. A baby's head can slump forward or sideways and obstruct breathing. Names such as pods, wedges or nests are not safety guarantees; choose a flat sleep space that meets federal infant sleep-product standards.714

寶寶在汽車座椅或搖椅上睡著了呢？按說明使用的搖椅或彈跳椅不是睡床。寶寶在行駛車內正確安裝、繫好安全帶的汽車座椅上打瞌睡通常可以；不要在車輛行進中解開束帶。安全停車後或抵達目的地，盡快將寶寶移到合規、堅固平坦的睡眠表面仰睡；不要把汽車座椅當成車外的睡床。71413

What if the baby falls asleep in a car seat or swing? Swings and bouncers used as directed are not sleep surfaces. Dozing in a properly installed and buckled car seat in a moving vehicle is generally okay; do not unbuckle the baby while the vehicle is moving. Once safely stopped or at your destination, move the baby onto their back on a compliant, firm, flat sleep surface as soon as possible. Do not use a car seat as a bed outside the vehicle.71413

家人用過的嬰兒床都沒出事，為什麼二手的要小心？家人用过的婴儿床都没出事，为什么二手的要小心？The family crib was fine for us. Why be careful with a used one?

因為太舊的床可能不符合安全標準。2011 年 6 月 28 日起，在美國製造或販售（含轉賣）的嬰兒床須符合新的聯邦標準，包括禁止傳統活動側欄設計、加強床板條、床墊支撐及五金。規定生效前的床需特別查證，不是所有二手床都不合格。12

Because very old cribs may not meet safety standards. Starting June 28, 2011, cribs manufactured or sold in U.S. commerce, including resale, had to meet new federal rules: traditional drop-side designs were prohibited and slats, mattress supports and hardware were strengthened. Verify older models particularly carefully; not all used cribs are noncompliant.12

規定是怎麼來的？CPSC 說，側欄會脫落的活動側欄嬰兒床，自 2000 年起至少和 32 起嬰兒窒息或勒斃死亡有關；2007 年起 CPSC 已召回超過 1,100 萬張危險嬰兒床（這是召回的床數，不是傷亡數），另有死亡是五金故障或瑕疵造成（以上是 CPSC 2011 年公布的數字）。2

规定是怎么来的？CPSC 说，侧栏会脱落的活动侧栏婴儿床，自 2000 年起至少和 32 起婴儿窒息或勒毙死亡有关；2007 年起 CPSC 已召回超过 1,100 万张危险婴儿床（这是召回的床数，不是伤亡数），另有死亡是五金故障或瑕疵造成（以上是 CPSC 2011 年公布的数字）。2

Where did the rules come from? CPSC says drop-side cribs with detaching side rails were associated with at least 32 infant suffocation and strangulation deaths since 2000, and it recalled more than 11 million dangerous cribs from 2007 (that counts cribs, not injuries). Other deaths came from faulty or defective hardware. (These are CPSC's 2011 figures.)2

買了之後還要寄註冊卡，真的有必要嗎？买了之后还要寄注册卡，真的有必要吗？Do I really need to send in the registration card?

有，這樣廠商召回時才找得到你。NHTSA（美國國家公路交通安全管理局）說，註冊汽車座椅後，製造商可以直接通知你召回和安全訊息；寄回隨附的卡片或到製造商網站登記都可以。CPSC 為了提高召回的效果，規定耐用型嬰幼兒產品要附註冊卡，並提供網站或電子郵件登記。910

有，这样厂商召回时才找得到你。NHTSA（美国国家公路交通安全管理局）说，注册儿童安全座椅后，制造商可以直接通知你召回和安全消息；寄回随附的卡片或到制造商网站登记都可以。CPSC 为了提高召回的效果，规定耐用型婴幼儿产品要附注册卡，并提供网站或电子邮件登记。910

Yes: it's how the maker finds you in a recall. NHTSA (the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration) says registering a car seat lets the manufacturer contact you directly about recalls and safety notices; mail the card that came with it or register on the maker's website. To make recalls work better, CPSC requires durable infant and toddler products to come with a registration card plus an online or email option.910

這些真的差很多嗎？这些真的差很多吗？How much does this matter?

睡眠環境是可以改善的一環。CDC 於 2026 年 8 月公布的資料顯示，2024 年美國約有 3,400 起一歲以下嬰兒突發非預期死亡（SUID），包括 1,351 起嬰兒猝死症（SIDS）、1,099 起死因不明、947 起床上意外窒息或勒頸死亡。這是全國死亡分類，不是由上述商品造成的死亡數，也不能用來計算單一商品的風險。12

The sleep environment is something families can improve. CDC's August 2026 update reports about 3,400 sudden unexpected infant deaths (SUID) in the U.S. in 2024 among infants under 1 year: 1,351 classified as SIDS, 1,099 as unknown cause, and 947 as accidental suffocation or strangulation in bed. These are national cause categories, not deaths attributed to the products above or estimates of the risk from a single product.12
