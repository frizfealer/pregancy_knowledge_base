胎毛與白色胎脂：健康足月寶寶的第一次洗澡，建議等至少 24 小時Lanugo and vernix: wait at least 24 hours for the first bath of a healthy term newborn

胎毛約在孕中期長出，幫胎脂留在皮膚上，多半在出生前脫落。胎脂有保濕與保護作用。WHO 2022 年指引建議健康足月新生兒至少等 24 小時再洗第一次澡；若有感染暴露、早產或其他醫療需要，由醫療團隊決定。回家後先擦澡，直到臍帶脫落且肚臍癒合。Lanugo develops in mid-pregnancy, helps retain vernix on the skin and usually sheds before birth. Vernix helps protect and moisturize the skin. WHO's 2022 guidance recommends delaying the first bath of a healthy term newborn for at least 24 hours; infection exposure, prematurity or other medical needs require an individualized care plan. At home, use sponge baths until the cord falls off and the navel heals.

先猜一猜Guess first
寶寶在子宮裡會長出一身細細的胎毛。出生的時候，這層毛通常還在嗎？In the womb your baby grows a coat of fine, downy hair. Is it usually still there at birth?

通常不在了。胎毛多半在出生前就脫落；足月寶寶仍可能留有一些，早產的寶寶則更常見。12

Usually not. Most lanugo sheds before birth, although some term babies and more premature babies still have it.12

第一步・白話版Step 1 · The short version

細毛加胎脂，幫助保護胎兒皮膚Fine hair and vernix help protect fetal skin

孕中期，胎兒會長出一層細毛，稱為胎毛（lanugo）。它幫助胎脂（vernix，一層白色蠟質物質）留在皮膚上，保護皮膚免於過度接觸羊水，並可能有助於保溫。1234

During the second trimester, the fetus develops fine hair called lanugo. It helps retain vernix, a white waxy coating that protects skin against excessive water exposure; it may also contribute to temperature regulation.1234

出生時寶寶身上可能還有白色胎脂，AAP 建議暫時保留。WHO 2022 年第 30 項建議適用於健康足月新生兒：出生後至少等 24 小時再洗第一次澡。AAP 對某些感染暴露另有建議，請由醫療團隊安排。568

Some white vernix may remain at birth; AAP advises leaving it on for a while. WHO's 2022 recommendation 30 applies to healthy term newborns: wait at least 24 hours. AAP has separate advice for certain infection exposures; follow the care team's plan.568

第二步・原理Step 2 · Why

每一件事，都多問一句「為什麼」One more "why" for each

寶寶泡在溫暖的羊水裡，為什麼還要長毛？The baby floats in warm fluid, so why grow hair at all?

主要是為了固定胎脂。克里夫蘭診所說，胎毛把胎脂留在皮膚上，兩者一起有助於保溫、保護皮膚。 NHS 的說法比較保留：胎毛「可能」是用來讓寶寶維持穩定的體溫。23

Mostly to hold the vernix in place. Cleveland Clinic says lanugo keeps vernix on the skin, and together they help insulate the body and protect the skin. The NHS is more cautious, saying lanugo is probably there to keep the baby at a steady temperature.23

胎脂到底是什麼？為什麼泡在水裡的皮膚需要它？What is vernix, and why does skin that lives in water need it?

它是一層具疏水性的保護膜。一篇 2019 年的研究回顧指出，胎脂約含 80% 水、10% 蛋白質、10% 脂質，具疏水性（排斥水），能防止皮膚泡水過度。 出生後，它也幫寶寶從水中過渡到空氣中：防止水分流失、調節體溫，並參與先天免疫。4

It's a water-repelling protective layer. A 2019 research review describes vernix as about 80% water, 10% protein and 10% lipids. It is hydrophobic (it repels water) and protects the skin from too much water exposure. After birth it helps with the move from water to air: it limits water loss, helps regulate temperature and plays a part in innate immunity.4

白白黏黏的，不是應該馬上洗乾淨才衛生嗎？It looks messy. Isn't washing it off right away more hygienic?

通常不用急，但感染暴露要另行評估。AAP 家長網站建議暫時保留胎脂，以避免乾燥。AAP 2022 年臨床報告對 HIV、B 型或 C 型肝炎病毒，以及活動性生殖器疱疹暴露建議出生後儘早清洗；WHO 2022 年與美國 NIH 2025 年 HIV 指引均指出缺乏證據證明提早洗澡能阻斷感染或需要改變常規洗澡時間。因此不能自行套用 24 小時或等待第一次哺乳的規則，由新生兒醫療團隊按暴露情況安排。5689

Usually no rush, but infection exposure needs separate assessment.AAP's parent guidance recommends retaining vernix to reduce dryness. Its 2022 clinical report advises prompt bathing after exposure to HIV, hepatitis B or C, or active genital herpes. WHO 2022 and U.S. NIH 2025 HIV guidance note a lack of evidence for early bathing to prevent transmission or for changing routine bathing timing. Do not apply a fixed 24-hour or first-feed rule yourself; the newborn care team should individualize timing.5689

為什麼洗澡要等？早點洗會怎樣？Why wait for the bath? What happens if it's early?

剛出生的寶寶很容易著涼。AAP 說，一出生就洗澡的寶寶比較可能變冷、出現低體溫；早洗澡帶來的小小壓力，也可能讓某些寶寶血糖下降（低血糖）。5

Newborns get cold easily. AAP says babies bathed right away may be more likely to get cold and develop hypothermia, and the minor stress of an early bath can make some babies more likely to have a drop in blood sugar (hypoglycemia).5

等一等，真的差很多嗎？Does waiting really make much difference?

可能有幫助，但證據確定性偏低。2022 年系統性回顧納入 16 項研究、39,020 名健康足月或接近足月新生兒。事後分析比較出生後超過 6 小時（各研究為 9、12 或 24 小時起）與 6 小時內洗澡：低體溫勝算比 0.47（95% 信賴區間 0.36–0.61；4 項研究、2,711 人），低血糖勝算比 0.39（0.23–0.66；3 項研究、2,775 人）。這是勝算的相對差異，不是個人的絕對風險；多數證據確定性低。WHO 2022 年建議亦有系統性回顧作為依據，不只是專家共識。67

It may help, but certainty is low.A 2022 systematic review included 16 studies and 39,020 healthy term or near-term newborns. Its post-hoc comparison of bathing beyond six hours (at or after 9, 12 or 24 hours) versus within six hours found odds ratios of 0.47 for hypothermia (95% CI 0.36–0.61; four studies, 2,711 infants) and 0.39 for hypoglycemia (0.23–0.66; three studies, 2,775 infants). These are relative odds, not personal absolute risks; much of the evidence is low certainty. WHO's 2022 recommendation draws on a systematic review, not solely expert consensus.67

第三步・細節Step 3 · Details

出生後的頭幾週，怎麼洗Bathing in the first few weeks

  • 臍帶殘端掉之前只擦澡，通常在 1–2 週大時脫落；如果超過這個時間還沒掉，跟寶寶的醫師談談。5
  • 肚臍處癒合之後，可嘗試短時間泡澡。洗澡時始終有成人在旁並用手扶住寶寶，不要讓寶寶獨處。5
  • 紐約 NewYork-Presbyterian 的公開照護說明採用出生後延後 24 小時洗澡；各院與個別醫療情況可能不同。10
  • 新生兒不需要每天洗澡。第一年一週洗 3 次可能就夠了，洗太勤反而會讓皮膚變乾。5
  • 胎毛開始的時間，各來源說法不同：MedlinePlus 寫 15–18 週先長在頭上、22 週覆蓋全身；克里夫蘭診所寫 16–20 週長出。12
  • 出生時若身上還有胎毛，特別是早產兒，是正常的；通常在出生後數週內脫落；若持續不退可詢問醫師。2
  • Give only sponge baths until the umbilical cord stump falls off, which usually happens by one to two weeks of age. If it stays on longer, talk with your baby's doctor.5
  • Once the navel has healed, try brief tub baths. An adult must keep a hand on the baby and never leave the baby alone in the bath.5
  • NewYork-Presbyterian publicly describes delaying newborn bathing for 24 hours; hospital policies and individual medical needs may vary.10
  • Newborns don't need a daily bath. Three baths a week in the first year may be enough, and bathing more often can dry out the skin.5
  • Sources differ on when lanugo starts: MedlinePlus says it appears on the head in weeks 15–18 and covers the whole body by week 22; Cleveland Clinic says it develops between weeks 16 and 20.12
  • Lanugo at birth is normal, especially in premature babies, and it generally sheds within weeks; ask the pediatrician if it persists.2

今天就能做

Do today

  • 在生產計畫中註明：健康足月且情況允許時，希望至少等 24 小時再洗第一次澡。
  • 參觀醫院或下次產檢時，問醫療團隊常規洗澡時間及例外。
  • 記下來：回家後先擦澡，等臍帶掉了、肚臍好了再泡澡，不用每天洗。
  • In the birth plan, request a first bath after at least 24 hours if the baby is healthy, term and the care team agrees.
  • Ask the hospital about routine first-bath timing and medical exceptions.
  • Note for later: at home, sponge baths until the cord falls off and the belly button heals, then tub baths, and not every day.

胎兒為什麼要長胎毛？Why does a fetus grow lanugo?

胎毛幫助胎脂留在皮膚上，保護皮膚免於過度接觸羊水，也可能有助於保溫。

Lanugo helps retain vernix, which protects skin against excessive water exposure; it may also help with temperature regulation.

為什麼新生兒第一次洗澡要等？等多久？Why wait to give a newborn the first bath, and for how long?

延後洗澡可能減少低體溫及低血糖，胎脂也能保濕。WHO 2022 年建議健康足月新生兒至少等 24 小時；感染暴露或其他醫療需要由醫療團隊決定。

Delaying bathing may reduce hypothermia and hypoglycemia, and vernix helps moisturize skin. WHO 2022 recommends at least 24 hours for healthy term newborns; the care team decides timing when infection exposure or other medical needs apply.

新生兒什麼時候可以開始泡澡？When can a newborn have a tub bath?

臍帶殘端掉了（通常 1–2 週）、肚臍癒合之後。之前只擦澡。

After the cord stump falls off (usually by 1–2 weeks) and the belly button heals. Until then, sponge baths only.

寶寶出生時肩膀上還有細毛，需要擔心嗎？Your baby is born with fine hair on the shoulders. Is that a worry?

通常不用。足月寶寶有時也會留有胎毛，早產兒更常見；通常數週內自行脫落，若持續不退可詢問醫師。

Usually not. Some term babies and more premature babies still have lanugo. It generally sheds within weeks; ask the pediatrician if it persists.
