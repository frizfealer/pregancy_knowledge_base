寶寶這幾週開始長細毛，再裹上一層白色胎脂：出生後第一次洗澡，可以等 24 小時Your baby is starting to grow fine hair, then a waxy white coat: after birth, the first bath can wait 24 hours

胎毛約在孕中期長出，幫胎脂黏在皮膚上，一起替寶寶保溫、防水，多半在出生前脫落。胎脂有保濕作用，WHO 建議第一次洗澡等到出生 24 小時後，做不到至少等 6 小時，可降低著涼和低血糖的機會；臍帶掉之前只擦澡。Lanugo grows in the second trimester and holds vernix on the skin, keeping the baby warm and waterproofed; most of it sheds before birth. Vernix moisturizes, and WHO recommends waiting 24 hours for the first bath, or at least 6, which may lower the chance of getting cold or low blood sugar. Sponge baths only until the cord falls off.

先猜一猜Guess first
寶寶在子宮裡會長出一身細細的胎毛。出生的時候，這層毛通常還在嗎？In the womb your baby grows a coat of fine, downy hair. Is it usually still there at birth?

通常不在了。胎毛多半在出生前就掉了，足月時大多只剩上臂和肩膀還有一些。 早產的寶寶比較常帶著胎毛出生，這是正常的。12

Usually not: lanugo typically sheds before birth, and at full term it's gone except on the upper arms and shoulders. Babies born early are more likely to still have it, and that's normal.12

第一步・白話版Step 1 · The short version

細毛加胎脂，是寶寶在羊水裡的「防水外套」Fine hair plus vernix: your baby's raincoat in the amniotic fluid

孕中期，胎兒開始長出一層很細的毛，叫「胎毛」（lanugo）。它的作用是保護和保暖，還能讓「胎脂」（vernix，一層像乳酪、蠟質的白色物質）黏在皮膚上。 胎脂保護寶寶的皮膚不被羊水泡壞。2

In the second trimester, a fetus starts growing a layer of very fine hair called lanugo. It's there for protection and warmth, and it helps vernix (a waxy, cheese-like white coating) stick to the skin. Vernix shields the baby's skin from the amniotic fluid it floats in.2

出生時寶寶身上可能還有白色胎脂。美國兒科學會（AAP）建議先讓胎脂留在皮膚上一段時間；世界衛生組織（WHO）2022 年指引建議健康足月新生兒的第一次洗澡至少延後至出生 24 小時後。早產、病況不穩定或有感染暴露的寶寶，由醫療團隊決定時程。568

Some vernix may remain at birth. The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) advises leaving it on for a while; the World Health Organization (WHO) 2022 guideline recommends delaying the first bath of healthy term newborns until at least 24 hours after birth. For premature or unwell babies, or those with infectious exposures, the care team decides the timing.568

第二步・原理Step 2 · Why

每一件事，都多問一句「為什麼」One more "why" for each

寶寶泡在溫暖的羊水裡，為什麼還要長毛？The baby floats in warm fluid, so why grow hair at all?

主要是為了固定胎脂。克里夫蘭診所說，胎毛把胎脂留在皮膚上，兩者一起有助於保溫、保護皮膚。 NHS 的說法比較保留：胎毛「可能」是用來讓寶寶維持穩定的體溫。23

Mostly to hold the vernix in place. Cleveland Clinic says lanugo keeps vernix on the skin, and together they help insulate the body and protect the skin. The NHS is more cautious, saying lanugo is probably there to keep the baby at a steady temperature.23

胎脂到底是什麼？為什麼泡在水裡的皮膚需要它？What is vernix, and why does skin that lives in water need it?

它是一層防水膜。一篇 2019 年的研究回顧指出，胎脂約含 80% 水、10% 蛋白質、10% 脂質，具疏水性（不吸水），能防止皮膚泡水過度。 出生後，它也幫寶寶從水中過渡到空氣中：防止水分流失、調節體溫，並參與先天免疫。4

It's a waterproof layer. A 2019 research review describes vernix as about 80% water, 10% protein and 10% lipids. It is hydrophobic (it repels water) and protects the skin from too much water exposure. After birth it helps with the move from water to air: it limits water loss, helps regulate temperature and plays a part in innate immunity.4

白白黏黏的，不是應該馬上洗乾淨才衛生嗎？It looks messy. Isn't washing it off right away more hygienic?

不用急，胎脂有保濕作用。AAP 家長網站也指出胎脂可能具有抗菌作用。美國 AAP 2022 年臨床報告建議，若新生兒有 HIV、B 型或 C 型肝炎病毒暴露，或分娩時接觸活動性生殖器疱疹病灶，應由醫療團隊安排出生後儘早洗澡，不應為等待哺乳或滿 24 小時而延誤。WHO 指出缺乏早洗以預防母嬰感染的直接證據；洗澡不能取代其他感染預防與治療。568

There is no rush; vernix moisturizes. AAP's parenting site also notes possible antibacterial properties. A 2022 AAP clinical report recommends bathing as soon as possible after birth for newborns exposed to HIV, hepatitis B or C, or active genital herpes lesions. The care team should decide timing rather than waiting for a feed or 24 hours. WHO notes a lack of direct evidence for early bathing to prevent maternal transmission; bathing does not replace other infection prevention or treatment.568

為什麼洗澡要等？早點洗會怎樣？Why wait for the bath? What happens if it's early?

剛出生的寶寶很容易著涼。AAP 說，一出生就洗澡的寶寶比較可能變冷、出現低體溫；早洗澡帶來的小小壓力，也可能讓某些寶寶血糖下降（低血糖）。5

Newborns get cold easily. AAP says babies bathed right away may be more likely to get cold and develop hypothermia, and the minor stress of an early bath can make some babies more likely to have a drop in blood sugar (hypoglycemia).5

等一等，真的差很多嗎？Does waiting really make much difference?

可能有幫助，但證據不算強。2022 年系統性回顧納入 16 項研究、39,020 名足月或接近足月的健康新生兒。事後分析比較出生後 6 小時內洗澡與較晚洗澡（各研究採 9、12 或 24 小時等時點）：低體溫的勝算比為 0.47（4 項研究、2,711 名新生兒），低血糖的勝算比為 0.39（3 項研究、2,775 名新生兒）。這是相對勝算，不是絕對風險下降，也不能證明剛滿 6 小時有相同效果。多數證據確定性偏低；WHO 2022 年的 24 小時建議已納入系統性回顧證據。67

It probably helps, though the evidence is limited. A 2022 systematic review included 16 studies and 39,020 healthy term or near-term newborns. A post-hoc analysis compared bathing within 6 hours with later baths (at 9, 12 or 24 hours, depending on the study). The odds ratios were 0.47 for hypothermia (4 studies, 2,711 newborns) and 0.39 for low blood sugar (3 studies, 2,775 newborns). These are relative odds, not absolute risk reductions, and do not establish the same benefit at exactly 6 hours. Most evidence was low certainty; the WHO 2022 recommendation incorporates systematic-review evidence.67

第三步・細節Step 3 · Details

出生後的頭幾週，怎麼洗Bathing in the first few weeks

  • 臍帶殘端掉之前只擦澡，通常在 1–2 週大時脫落；如果超過這個時間還沒掉，跟寶寶的醫師談談。5
  • 肚臍處癒合之後，就可以試著把寶寶直接放進水裡洗。5
  • 新生兒不需要每天洗澡。第一年一週洗 3 次可能就夠了，洗太勤反而會讓皮膚變乾。5
  • 胎毛開始的時間，各來源說法不同：MedlinePlus 寫 15–18 週先長在頭上、22 週覆蓋全身；克里夫蘭診所寫 16–20 週長出。12
  • 出生時若身上還有胎毛，特別是早產兒，是正常的；它通常在出生後頭兩個月內消失。2
  • Give only sponge baths until the umbilical cord stump falls off, which usually happens by one to two weeks of age. If it stays on longer, talk with your baby's doctor.5
  • Once the belly button area has healed, you can try placing your baby directly in the water.5
  • Newborns don't need a daily bath. Three baths a week in the first year may be enough, and bathing more often can dry out the skin.5
  • Sources differ on when lanugo starts: MedlinePlus says it appears on the head in weeks 15–18 and covers the whole body by week 22; Cleveland Clinic says it develops between weeks 16 and 20.12
  • Lanugo at birth is normal, especially in premature babies, and it disappears within the first two months of life.2

今天就能做

Do today

  • 在生產計畫的筆記裡加一行：「若寶寶健康足月且醫療團隊同意，首次洗澡至少延後 24 小時」。
  • 參觀醫院或下次產檢時，問一句：寶寶出生後多久洗第一次澡？可以等嗎？
  • 記下來：回家後先擦澡，等臍帶掉了、肚臍好了再泡澡，不用每天洗。
  • Add one line to your birth-plan notes: "If the baby is healthy and term and the care team agrees, delay the first bath for at least 24 hours."
  • On the hospital tour or at your next visit, ask: when do you usually give the first bath, and can we wait?
  • Note for later: at home, sponge baths until the cord falls off and the belly button heals, then tub baths, and not every day.

胎兒為什麼要長胎毛？Why does a fetus grow lanugo?

為了保護和保暖：胎毛讓胎脂黏在皮膚上，一起替身體保溫、保護皮膚不被羊水泡壞。

For protection and warmth: it holds vernix on the skin, and together they insulate the body and shield the skin from the amniotic fluid.

為什麼新生兒第一次洗澡要等？等多久？Why wait to give a newborn the first bath, and for how long?

早洗可能增加低體溫和低血糖的機會；胎脂能保濕。WHO 2022 年建議健康足月兒至少等 24 小時；有感染暴露或特殊病況時由醫療團隊決定。

Early bathing may increase the chance of hypothermia and low blood sugar; vernix moisturizes the skin. WHO 2022 recommends at least 24 hours for healthy term babies; the care team decides timing for infectious exposures or special medical circumstances.

新生兒什麼時候可以開始泡澡？When can a newborn have a tub bath?

臍帶殘端掉了（通常 1–2 週）、肚臍癒合之後。之前只擦澡。

After the cord stump falls off (usually by 1–2 weeks) and the belly button heals. Until then, sponge baths only.

寶寶出生時肩膀上還有細毛，需要擔心嗎？Your baby is born with fine hair on the shoulders. Is that a worry?

不用。足月時上臂和肩膀常還留一些，早產兒更常見；通常出生後兩個月內就會消失。

No. At term some often remains on the upper arms and shoulders, more so in premature babies, and it usually disappears within two months.
