It's a waterproof layer. A 2019 research review describes vernix as about 80% water, 10% protein and 10% lipids. It is hydrophobic (it repels water) and protects the skin from too much water exposure. After birth it helps with the move from water to air: it limits water loss, helps regulate temperature and plays a part in innate immunity.4

There's no rush, and it's good for the skin. 為什麼洗澡要等？早點洗會怎樣？为什么洗澡要等？早点洗会怎样？Why wait for the bath? What happens if it's early?

剛出生的寶寶很容易著涼。AAP 說，一出生就洗澡的寶寶比較可能變冷、出現低體溫；早洗澡帶來的小小壓力，也可能讓某些寶寶血糖下降（低血糖）。5

刚出生的宝宝很容易着凉。AAP 说，一出生就洗澡的宝宝比较可能变冷、出现低体温；早洗澡带来的小小压力，也可能让某些宝宝血糖下降（低血糖）。5

Newborns get cold easily. AAP says babies bathed right away may be more likely to get cold and develop hypothermia, and the minor stress of an early bath can make some babies more likely to have a drop in blood sugar (hypoglycemia).5

等一等，真的差很多嗎？等一等，真的差很多吗？Does waiting really make much difference?

可能有幫助，但證據不算強。一篇 2022 年的系統性回顧納入 16 項研究、39,020 名足月或接近足月的健康新生兒。和更早洗澡相比，至少等 6 小時，低體溫的勝算比為 0.47（4 項研究、2,711 名新生兒），低血糖的勝算比為 0.39（3 項研究、2,775 名新生兒），也就是發生的勝算大約少一半以上。 作者也提醒，多數結論的證據確定性偏低。 WHO 的 24 小時建議本身，是依據專家共識而定。67

It probably helps, though the evidence isn't strong. A 2022 systematic review covered 16 studies and 39,020 healthy term or near-term newborns. Compared with earlier bathing, waiting at least 6 hours was linked to lower odds of hypothermia (odds ratio 0.47; 4 studies, 2,711 newborns) and of low blood sugar (odds ratio 0.39; 3 studies, 2,775 newborns), roughly halving the odds or better. The authors note that most of these conclusions rest on low-certainty evidence. WHO's 24-hour recommendation itself is based on expert consensus.67
