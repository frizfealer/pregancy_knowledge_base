孕期睡不著，別在床上硬躺：最有證據的解法是一套不吃藥的練習Can't sleep in pregnancy? Don't just lie there: the best-studied fix is a drug-free routine

孕期失眠很常見，到孕晚期更多，產後也不一定自己好。失眠的認知行為治療（CBT-I）教你記睡眠日記、固定起床時間、睡不著就離床、暫時縮短躺床時間；在 179 名孕婦的試驗中，5 次療程後 64% 緩解，對照組 52%。Insomnia is common in pregnancy, more so in the third trimester, and doesn't always end at birth. CBT-I teaches a sleep diary, a fixed wake time, getting up when you can't sleep and briefly limiting time in bed; in a trial of 179 pregnant women, 64% were in remission after five sessions versus 52% with the control.

先猜一猜Guess first
治療失眠最有證據的非藥物方法，其中一步是「先少躺一點」。是真的嗎？One step of the best-studied drug-free insomnia treatment is to spend less time in bed. True?

是真的，但這是有專業指導的失眠治療，不是鼓勵孕婦少睡。睡眠限制依睡眠日記調整躺床時間，讓睡眠更集中；孕期不應自行縮短睡眠時間。5

Yes, but this is clinician-guided insomnia treatment, not advice to sleep less during pregnancy. Sleep restriction adjusts time in bed using a diary to consolidate sleep; do not reduce sleep on your own during pregnancy.5

第一步・白話版Step 1 · The short version

孕期睡不好很常見，但失眠有一套不用吃藥、有試驗支持的練習Poor sleep is common in pregnancy, and insomnia has a drug-free, trial-tested fix

肚子變大後不好躺、膀胱受壓要起來上廁所、做奇怪的夢，都會讓孕期睡不好。12

A growing bump that makes lying down uncomfortable, a squashed bladder that sends you to the toilet, and strange dreams all make sleep harder in pregnancy.12

一項涵蓋 24 項研究、15,564 人的統合分析估計，孕早期、孕中期及孕晚期的失眠症狀比例分別為 25.3%、27.2% 和 39.7%。這是症狀估計，不等於醫師診斷的失眠症。3

A meta-analysis of 24 studies with 15,564 participants estimated insomnia symptoms in 25.3%, 27.2% and 39.7% in the first, second and third trimesters, respectively. These are symptom estimates, not rates of diagnosed insomnia disorder.3

對慢性失眠，美國睡眠醫學會支持失眠的認知行為治療（CBT-I），透過調整睡眠習慣及想法改善失眠。52019 年孕婦試驗隨機分配 194 人，179 人納入分析；療程結束時 CBT-I 組 64% 緩解，對照組 52%。4

For chronic insomnia, CBT-I is an evidence-based treatment that addresses sleep habits and thoughts.5A 2019 pregnancy trial randomized 194 participants and analyzed 179; remission at the end of treatment was 64% with CBT-I and 52% with control.4

第二步・原理Step 2 · Why

每一件事，都多問一句「為什麼」One more "why" for each

懷孕本來就睡不好，忍到生完不就好了？Pregnancy sleep is just bad. Why not wait it out until the birth?

生完不一定會好。挪威一項 2,088 人的研究中，自述睡眠效率由孕 32 週約 84% 降至產後 8 週約 75%，但失眠評分及失眠比例反而下降。睡得更少不一定等於失眠症更嚴重。6

Insomnia does not necessarily end at birth. In a Norwegian cohort of 2,088 women, self-reported sleep efficiency fell from about 84% at 32 weeks to 75% at eight weeks postpartum, while insomnia scores and prevalence decreased. Less sleep is not necessarily more severe insomnia.6

孕期失眠會變成產後憂鬱嗎？Does insomnia in pregnancy lead to postpartum depression?

研究結論不一致。芬蘭一項 2,224 名女性的研究發現，孕早期要 20 分鐘以上才睡著、孕晚期睡眠不足，都能預測產後 3 個月的憂鬱症狀。7但上面那項挪威研究發現，對過去沒有憂鬱病史的女性，孕 32 週的失眠並不能預測產後憂鬱。6這些觀察性研究不能證明失眠造成產後憂鬱。失眠如果伴隨絕望感、對原本喜歡的事失去興趣，可能是憂鬱的徵兆，應該告訴醫師或助產師。1

The studies disagree. A Finnish study of 2,224 women found that taking 20 minutes or more to fall asleep in early pregnancy, and not getting enough sleep in late pregnancy, predicted depressive symptoms 3 months after the birth.7 But the Norwegian study above found that, in women with no history of depression, insomnia at 32 weeks did not predict postpartum depression.6 These observational studies cannot establish that insomnia causes postpartum depression. Sleeplessness that comes with feeling hopeless or losing interest in things you used to enjoy can be a sign of depression, and is worth raising with your doctor or midwife.1

已經很累了，為什麼還要少躺、睡不著還要下床？I'm already exhausted. Why spend less time in bed, and get up when I can't sleep?

睡眠限制能加強睡意，但也可能使白天更睏、注意力下降。刺激控制通常包括想睡才上床、睡不著時在安全情況下離床、床只用於睡覺與性生活、固定起床時間等。睡眠限制及午睡安排應由治療師依孕期疲倦與安全需求調整，不要自行減少睡眠機會或在睏倦時開車。5

Limiting time in bed can increase sleep drive but may also worsen daytime sleepiness and concentration. Stimulus control includes going to bed when sleepy, leaving the bed if unable to sleep when safe, using the bed only for sleep and sex, and keeping a regular wake time. A clinician should tailor sleep restriction and naps to pregnancy fatigue and safety; do not reduce sleep opportunity on your own or drive while sleepy.5

睡前不喝咖啡、不滑手機，還不夠嗎？Isn't cutting coffee and screens before bed enough?

對長期失眠通常不夠。美國睡眠醫學會的指引不建議只靠「睡眠衛生」（避免咖啡因、規律作息這類好習慣）當作唯一治療。5在孕婦試驗裡，對照組接受的是以意象練習為主的主動對照治療，而非完全不治療，結果改善得比較少。4

For chronic insomnia, usually not. The American Academy of Sleep Medicine guideline does not recommend sleep hygiene (good habits such as avoiding caffeine and keeping regular hours) as a treatment on its own.5 In the pregnancy trial, the active control used imagery exercises rather than no treatment, and improved less.4

CBT-I 對孕婦到底有多大幫助？How much does CBT-I actually help in pregnancy?

失眠嚴重度下降，好轉也較快。2019 年試驗納入孕 18–32 週、符合失眠診斷條件的受試者，排除重度憂鬱等精神疾病或正在接受會影響睡眠的其他治療者；194 人隨機分配，179 人有基線資料並納入分析（CBT-I 89 人、對照 90 人）。失眠嚴重度指數由 15.4 降到 8.0 分、對照組由 15.9 降到 11.2 分；緩解（低於 8 分）比例為 64% 對 52%，中位緩解時間為 31 天對 48 天。自述夜間清醒改善較多，但感測器測量未顯示組間差異。4

Insomnia severity improved and remission came sooner. The 2019 trial enrolled participants at 18–32 weeks meeting insomnia criteria, excluding major psychiatric disorders including depression or other treatment affecting sleep. Of 194 randomized, 179 had baseline data and were analyzed (89 CBT-I, 90 control). Insomnia Severity Index scores fell from 15.4 to 8.0 versus 15.9 to 11.2; remission (score below 8) was 64% versus 52%, with median time to remission of 31 versus 48 days. Self-reported nighttime wakefulness improved more, but sensor-measured wakefulness did not differ between groups.4

不只一項試驗支持 CBT-I。2023 年統合分析納入 8 項隨機試驗、743 名孕婦，失眠嚴重度指數平均比對照低 4.25 分（95% 信賴區間：低 2.19–6.32 分），但總睡眠時間和睡眠效率的組間差異未達統計顯著。不能保證每個人睡得更久。9

More than one trial supports CBT-I. A 2023 meta-analysis of eight randomized trials involving 743 pregnant women found Insomnia Severity Index scores averaged 4.25 points lower than controls (95% CI: 2.19–6.32 points lower), but total sleep time and sleep efficiency did not differ significantly. Longer sleep is not guaranteed for every patient.9

為什麼現在孕中期就值得處理？Why deal with it now, in the second trimester?

孕晚期失眠症狀更常見，而治療需要時間。孕晚期症狀估計約 40%。3試驗收的是 18–32 週的孕婦，並未建立最佳治療起始孕週。只要失眠持續或影響日間功能，就值得與產科團隊討論。4

Symptoms are more common in late pregnancy and treatment takes time. Third-trimester symptom prevalence was estimated at about 40%.3The trial enrolled at 18–32 weeks and did not establish an ideal starting week. Persistent insomnia or daytime impairment warrants discussing it with your obstetric team.4

第三步・細節Step 3 · Details

日常小撇步、說法不一的地方，以及何時要找醫師Everyday tips, where advice differs, and when to call

要不要午睡？各家說法不同Should you nap? Sources differ

英國 NHS 建議避免下午或傍晚午睡；一般成人的刺激控制也包含不午睡。15愛爾蘭 HSE 則建議孕晚期睡不好時適當補休。2兩者針對不同目的：孕期疲倦與慢性失眠。不要把偶爾睡不好不必擔心的安慰理解為長期睡眠不足一定無害；午睡及睡眠限制依個人狀況由治療師調整。5

The NHS advises against afternoon or evening naps; standard adult stimulus control also discourages naps.15Ireland's HSE recommends daytime rest when sleep is poor in late pregnancy.2These address different goals: pregnancy fatigue and chronic insomnia. Reassurance about occasional poor sleep is not proof that persistent sleep deprivation is harmless; tailor naps and sleep restriction with a clinician.5

兩國衛生單位都同意的小撇步Tips both health services give

  • 傍晚以後避開茶、咖啡、可樂等含咖啡因飲料。12
  • 睡前幾小時讓自己放鬆；HSE 也建議睡前一小時不要用手機、平板或筆電。12
  • 白天喝足水，避免睡前一次喝大量的水，減少夜間如廁。2
  • 側睡，用枕頭撐住肚子或夾在兩腿之間。28 週以後，仰躺入睡與較高的死產風險有關；半夜醒來發現仰睡，翻回側面就好。12
  • 白天適度活動，例如午休散步或游泳，可以讓人比較不累。1
  • Avoid tea, coffee and cola in the evening.12
  • Wind down in the hours before bed; the HSE also suggests no phones, tablets or laptops in the last hour.12
  • Stay hydrated during the day and avoid large amounts just before bed to reduce nighttime bathroom trips.2
  • Sleep on your side, with a pillow under the bump or between your knees. After 28 weeks, going to sleep on your back is linked to a higher risk of stillbirth; if you wake up on your back, just roll onto your side.12
  • Stay active in the day, such as a lunchtime walk or a swim, which can help you feel less tired.1

什麼時候要找醫師When to call your doctor

  • 睡不著又覺得絕望、對以前喜歡的事失去興趣：可能是憂鬱，有治療可以幫忙。1
  • 疲倦持續可請醫師評估缺鐵等原因；若突然極度疲倦、無力維持日常活動，應立即就醫。28
  • 胸痛、呼吸困難、昏厥、持續頭暈、心跳異常快或有傷害自己或寶寶的想法：依美國 CDC 指引立即就醫，不要等下次產檢。8若有嚴重胸痛、嚴重呼吸困難、失去意識或立即傷害自己或他人的危險，請撥美國 911。10
  • 睡眠限制不適合需要高度警覺的工作者，也不適合容易躁症或癲癇發作的人；這類情況要和醫師討論替代做法。5
  • Sleeplessness with feeling hopeless or losing interest in things you enjoyed: this can be depression, and treatment helps.1
  • Persistent fatigue warrants evaluation for causes such as low iron; sudden overwhelming fatigue or inability to manage daily activities warrants immediate care.28
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, persistent dizziness, a racing heart or thoughts of harming yourself or your baby: seek medical care immediately under US CDC guidance; do not wait for your next prenatal visit.8 Call 911 in the US for severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness or imminent danger of harm to yourself or others.10
  • Sleep restriction is not advised for people in safety-critical jobs or prone to mania or seizures; talk with your doctor about alternatives.5

今天就能做

Do today

  • 從今晚開始記 1–2 週睡眠日記：幾點上床、大約多久睡著、半夜醒幾次、幾點起床。
  • 先固定一個每天的起床時間，週末也一樣。
  • 如果睡不好持續或影響白天，儘早聯絡產科團隊詢問 CBT-I 轉介，並提供睡眠日記。
  • 不要自己把躺床時間砍到很短；睡眠限制請在醫師或治療師指導下做。
  • Start a 1–2 week sleep diary tonight: bedtime, roughly how long it takes to fall asleep, night wakings, and wake-up time.
  • Pick one fixed wake-up time for every day, weekends included.
  • If poor sleep persists or affects daytime functioning, contact your obstetric team soon about a CBT-I referral and share the diary.
  • Don't cut your time in bed drastically on your own; do sleep restriction with guidance from a clinician or therapist.
